Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Flower Agency ("we") runs the YouTube channels Foundry, Forge, De Gieterij, Morocco 2040 and Starship Orbit. To manage these channels we use an internal studio tool ("the tool") that uses the YouTube API Services. This policy explains what the tool does with data.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our channels you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the tool

Only our own team. The tool is not available to the public, and it only connects to YouTube channels that we own.

What data the tool accesses

  • Our own channel and video information, so it can upload our own videos as private videos with their title, description and tags.
  • Public comments that viewers leave on our own videos, so we can read them and reply as the channel owner.
  • Aggregated statistics of our own channels (for example views and watch time), so we can improve our videos.

The tool does not access any other channels, private user data, contacts, payment data or Google account data of viewers.

How data is used and stored

Comment text and statistics are processed only to reply to viewers and to make an internal weekly report. Data is stored on our own computer, is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. Comment data is kept for at most 90 days; statistics are kept as long as they are useful for our reports.

Revoking access

The tool only has access to our own Google account. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the About page of any of our YouTube channels.
